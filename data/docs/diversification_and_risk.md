# Diversification and Risk Management
Diversification means spreading money across assets that do not move together, reducing the impact of any single holding. A common guideline is to keep no single stock above 5-10% of a portfolio.
## Concentration risk
Holding a large share of wealth in one company, including your employer's stock, exposes you to company-specific risk that diversification could remove.
## Asset allocation
Younger investors with long horizons typically hold more equities; those near retirement shift toward bonds and cash. Rebalancing annually restores target weights.
## Volatility
Individual stocks can fall 30% or more in a year even when the company is healthy. Position sizing should reflect how much loss an investor can tolerate.
