# Recognizing Suspicious Transactions
Common red flags: unusually large amounts compared with a customer's history, transactions at night (midnight to 5 AM), foreign merchants the customer has never used, and high-risk merchant categories such as crypto exchanges, gambling, gift cards and offshore wires.
## Patterns
Card testing involves several small charges followed by one large charge. Account takeover often shows a sudden jump in foreign activity.
## What to do
Contact the bank, freeze the card, change passwords, and dispute the charges promptly. Report suspected fraud within 30 days where possible.
