# Market Timing vs. Dollar-Cost Averaging
Research consistently shows that timing the market is difficult, even for professionals. Missing the ten best trading days in a decade can cut long-term returns by about half.
## Dollar-cost averaging (DCA)
DCA means investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, which reduces the risk of investing everything at a market peak. It lowers regret but does not guarantee higher returns than lump-sum investing.
## Indicators
Moving averages and one-month price change describe recent momentum but do not predict future returns. No indicator guarantees profit.
